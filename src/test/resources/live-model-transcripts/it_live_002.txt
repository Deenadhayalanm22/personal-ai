============================================================

Purpose
-------
Multilingual expense capture covering follow-ups, trusted buttons, multiple expenses in one turn,
read-only queries, and final ledger/session reconciliation. Live-model prose can vary except where
the test explicitly requires an exact reply.

Expense authorization
---------------------
After missing-field follow-ups are complete, and for every complete expense sentence, the app first
shows a structured preview. The test verifies no expense was persisted, presses Confirm, and only then
checks application and balances. The former two-expenses sentence is represented as two independently
previewed and confirmed messages so each financial write receives explicit authorization.
