ExpenseLiveIT.it_live_004 — EXPECTED CONVERSATION CONTRACT
============================================================

Purpose
-------
One monthly money-plan journey proving deterministic category summaries, persisted category budgets,
automatic warning/overspend alerts, card-due reminders, and read-only reconciliation. Live-model prose
may vary; persisted amounts and the required fragments below are the acceptance oracle.

Setup
-----
User: Create my HDFC salary bank account with a current balance of 20000
App must: confirm creation and mention HDFC.

User: Create my ICICI Coral credit card with limit 50000, outstanding 2500 and due day 12
App must: confirm creation and mention ICICI Coral.

Budget
------
User: Set my monthly groceries budget to 1000
App must: mention Groceries and 1000.
State: exactly one active Groceries monthly budget of ₹1,000.

Threshold alerts
----------------
User: I spent 850 on groceries using my HDFC salary bank account
App must: record ₹850 and warn that ₹150 is remaining because at least 80% is used.

User: I spent 300 on groceries using my HDFC salary bank account
App must: record ₹300 and say the Groceries budget is ₹150 over.

Monthly and budget queries
--------------------------
User: Give me my spending summary for this month by category
App must: mention total ₹1,150 and Groceries.

User: How am I doing against my budgets?
App must: mention Groceries, ₹1,150, and over budget.

Card reminders
--------------
User: Which credit card payments are due soon?
App must: mention ICICI Coral, ₹2,500 outstanding, and its calculated next due date on day 12.

Final oracle
------------
- Exactly two EXPENSE state changes and two corresponding balance mutations.
- Queries and reminders create no state changes or mutations.
- Exactly one active monthly budget exists.
- Conversation has no active intent, waiting field, or pending events.
