ExpenseLiveIT.it_live_005 — REAL EXPENSE CONVERSATION CONTRACT
===============================================================

Purpose
-------
Reproduces a real user journey involving expense-specific help, provisional UPI setup, naturally
worded budgets, misspelled spending descriptions, budget tracking, and unsupported-message review.
Semantic interpretation may vary in prose, but persisted taxonomy and accounting are strict.

Help
----
User: Hi
App must: describe personal-expense features including capture, accounts, summaries, budgets, and reminders.

User: Help
App must: return the same enabled-extension-specific instructions.

UPI setup
---------
User: i spent 55 today morning for some snacks and i paid using upi
App must: create My bank account and request its current balance.

User: 10000
App must: apply ₹55 and report a ₹9,945 balance.

Budget
------
User: my grocery balance for this month is only 2000.
App must: persist a ₹2,000 monthly budget with canonical scope Groceries.

User: how much my grocery budget for this month
App must: answer budget status rather than returning only a generic monthly spending total.

AI taxonomy resolution
----------------------
User: i spent 1300 on the buying vegitables using my upi
App must: record ₹1,300. Persisted taxonomy must be Food & Dining / Groceries; arbitrary category
text such as "the buying vegitables" must not become a category key.

User: how am i doing my grocery budget against this month planned
App must: report ₹1,300 spent from ₹2,000 with ₹700 remaining.

User: i spent 800 on groceries today paid using upi
App must: record ₹800 and warn that Groceries is ₹100 over budget.

User: how much i spent today
App must: report ₹2,155 total and include Groceries in the breakdown.

Unsupported demand
------------------
User: Purple silence sideways banana orbit
App must: honestly say it could not understand and that the message was recorded for review.
State: one NEW unprocessed_conversation_message exists with exact text, WhatsApp message ID, and no movement.

User: help
App must: still return the expense-specific instructions.

Final oracle
------------
- Three applied expenses and three balance mutations: ₹55, ₹1,300, and ₹800.
- One canonical Groceries budget of ₹2,000.
- One idempotently stored unresolved message.
- Help and unsupported turns create no financial movement.
