ExpenseLiveIT.it_live_001 — EXPECTED CONVERSATION CONTRACT
============================================================

Purpose
-------
Two-month ledger journey across one salary bank account and three independently named credit cards.
App wording is generated by the live model and can vary. The expectations below describe the text
fragments and persisted accounting results enforced by the test; they are not fabricated exact replies.

Expense authorization
---------------------
For every purchase below, the app must first show amount, category, subcategory, and verified source.
The expense and its balance mutation must not exist until the user presses Confirm. The confirmation
reply then satisfies the existing recorded-expense and account-balance assertions.

Budget authorization
--------------------
Groceries and Eating Out budgets are previewed and confirmed after the first matching expense. The
Shopping budget is previewed and confirmed after the first Clothing expense establishes that user's
Shopping category. The first Clothing expense therefore has no budget warning; later final totals and
over-budget assertions remain unchanged.
