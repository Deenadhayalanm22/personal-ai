============================================================

Purpose
-------
Minimal explicit-UPI flow proving provisional account creation, opening-balance completion,
expense application, a read-only total, and clean conversation state.

Conversation
------------
User: I spent 50 on groceries today from my upi
App must: say it created My bank account and ask for its current balance.
State:
- One pending ₹50 expense
- Conversation waits for sourceBalance
- My bank account exists as BANK_ACCOUNT with unknown balance

User: 10k
App must: mention 50 and 9950.
State:
- ₹50 expense is financially applied
- My bank account balance is ₹9,950

User: What did I spend today?
App must: mention 50.
State: expense count remains one; the query creates no financial event.

Final accounting and session oracle
-----------------------------------
- Exactly one expense
- Exactly one state mutation
- No active conversation intent
- No waiting field
- No pending events
