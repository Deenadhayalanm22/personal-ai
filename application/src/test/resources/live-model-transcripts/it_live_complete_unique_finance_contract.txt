ExpenseLiveIT.it_live_complete_unique_finance_contract — MASTER CONTRACT
=======================================================================

Purpose
-------
One literal live-model test method executes all unique personal-finance acceptance behavior through
four isolated WhatsApp personas. The detailed phase contracts remain in it_live_001.txt through
it_live_005.txt and are incorporated here by reference.

Expense authorization (applies to every persona)
------------------------------------------------
Every expense-producing turn first returns a preview containing amount, category, subcategory, and
verified source (or null plus the detected unconfigured account). No expense or balance mutation may
exist at preview time. The conversation then sends the Confirm button before applying the existing
accounting oracle. An unconfigured source is set up only after confirmation; skipping setup preserves
the confirmed expense without a balance mutation. Discarded previews contribute no ledger entries.

Budget authorization (applies wherever budgets are configured)
----------------------------------------------------------------
A budget scope must already occur in that persona's confirmed expense history. The app previews the
monthly amount and category/subcategory relationship, writes nothing before Confirm, and saves only
after the Confirm button. Unknown user scopes instruct the user to record relevant expense data first.

Persona A — two-month ledger
----------------------------
Executes the complete it_live_001 contract, including named-card and generic all-container balance
queries. Final oracle: 12 state changes and 16 mutations with the documented account balances.

Persona B — multilingual conversation
--------------------------------------
Executes the complete it_live_002 contract: greeting, provisional account via payment button,
English/Tamil/Tanglish expenses, missing-field follow-ups, two independently confirmed expenses, balance query,
multilingual summaries, and clean-session reconciliation.

Persona C — explicit-UPI first use
----------------------------------
Executes the financial behavior from it_live_003 without repeating Persona B's generic greeting.
It must create a provisional bank account directly from explicit UPI wording, collect its opening
balance, apply the pending expense, answer a read-only total, and finish with a clean session.

Persona D — planning, alerts, and recovery
------------------------------------------
Executes it_live_004, then starts from a clean accounting state under the same fourth phone identity
and executes it_live_005. The reset is part of the contract: prior planning accounts and grocery
spend must not invalidate the real-user provisional-account and budget journey.

Isolation oracle
----------------
- Exactly four phone identities are used.
- Repository and conversation state are reset between personas.
- Persona D is reset between its two incompatible clean-start phases.
- Every detailed persisted accounting, budget, routing, reminder, unsupported-message, and session
  assertion in the five referenced phase contracts remains enforced.
