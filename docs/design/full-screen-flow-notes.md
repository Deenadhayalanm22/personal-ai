# Money Stories full-screen concept

The concept is split into three boards:

1. Expense workspace: Home, Transactions, Edit transaction, Expense cleanup.
2. Optional money modules: Your money, Investments, Add investment, Planning forms.
3. Stories and control: unified feed, cross-money guidance, reasoning, privacy controls.

Expense cleanup is intentionally limited to AI-captured expense data. Investments, emergency funds, goals, and budgets use structured forms.
