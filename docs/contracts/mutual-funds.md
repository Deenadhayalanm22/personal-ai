# Mutual funds

Mutual-fund tracking selects a verified MFAPI scheme, maintains a holding and SIP occurrences, and calculates current valuation. `Home.svelte` owns the money-view flow.

| Endpoint | Request and response | Frontend owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/web/mutual-funds/search?q=text` | Required query. Array of `{ schemeCode, schemeName, latestNav }`. | Scheme picker; starts search at two characters. |
| `POST /api/web/mutual-funds` | `{ schemeCode, schemeName, isin?, monthlySipAmount?, sipDay?, startMonth?, existingHolding }`; SIP fields are either all supplied or all absent; the required holding is `{ currentUnits, totalInvestedAmount }` with both values positive. Returns summary with `201`. | Fund creation with an optional SIP and a required opening holding. |
| `POST /api/web/mutual-funds/{id}/sip` | `{ amount, day, startMonth }`. Returns the updated summary. | Set up a SIP later from Fund details for a fund without an active SIP. |
| `GET /api/web/mutual-funds` | `{ mutualFunds }`; a summary includes identifiers/name, invested/current value/P&L/latest NAV, and `activeSip?`. | Portfolio total and fund cards. |
| `GET /api/web/mutual-funds/{id}` | `{ id, schemeName, invested, currentValue, profitOrLoss, profitOrLossPercent, averageNav, currentNav, units, history }`; each chronological confirmed history item includes `id`, date, type, amount, NAV, units, calculation source, and `scheduledMonth` for SIP rows. | Fund-detail dialog and per-row correction controls. |
| `DELETE /api/web/mutual-funds/{id}` | Deletes the authenticated user's fund and all of its SIP, opening-holding, and lump-sum records; returns `204` and refreshes commitments. | Per-card delete icon. |
| `POST /api/web/mutual-funds/{id}/lump-sums` | `{ amount, transactionDate, nav?, units?, calculationSource }`; NAV or units is sufficient, returns confirmed transaction with `201`. | Fund-detail `＋ Lump sum` action asks for amount, date, and units received; NAV is derived. |
| `POST /api/web/mutual-funds/{id}/sip-occurrences/{month}/confirm` | Same body; month is `YYYY-MM`; returns confirmed occurrence. | SIP-due prompt sends `USER_ENTERED`, amount, date, and units received; NAV is derived. |
| `PATCH /api/web/mutual-funds/{id}/sip-occurrences/{month}` | Same body; corrects an already confirmed occurrence and returns its corrected allocation. | Mutual Funds section. |
| `PATCH /api/web/mutual-funds/{id}/transactions/{transactionId}` | Same body; corrects a confirmed opening holding, SIP, or lump sum and returns the corrected transaction. | Fund-detail history row. |

A fund requires a positive opening holding (units and invested amount); missing or zero values return `400 INVALID_MUTUAL_FUND`. A SIP requires amount, day 1–28, and start month, but it is optional when first creating a fund. A lump-sum-only fund can receive occasional lump sums and later receive one SIP through the SIP endpoint. A plan created during its requested start month first occurs in the following month, so adding a fund mid-month never creates a retroactive confirmation request. Otherwise creation adds the start-month occurrence; it is `SCHEDULED` before its configured local SIP day and becomes `DUE` on that day. Reading a user's mutual funds ensures the current-month occurrence exists and promotes an eligible scheduled occurrence to due, so a scheduler delay cannot hide a due SIP. Mutual-fund list entries expose their current SIP occurrence when one exists, including its month, status, allocation facts, and source. Confirmations require positive amount, date, a calculation source (`USER_ENTERED`, `NAV_ESTIMATED`, `STATEMENT_VERIFIED`), and NAV or units. Every confirmed opening holding, SIP, and lump sum can be corrected from its own Fund-detail history row; holdings are recalculated from confirmed transactions while the SIP plan remains unchanged. Only confirmed transactions contribute to invested value/units. Current valuation/P&L are `null` without latest NAV. Delete never exposes or removes another user's fund. Errors: `409 INVESTMENT_EXISTS`, `404 INVESTMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `404 INVESTMENT_TRANSACTION_NOT_FOUND`, `400 INVALID_MUTUAL_FUND`.

## SIP payment and plan revision

The frozen mutual-fund commitment journey is defined in `frontend/e2e/features/mutual-fund-commitment.feature`. Fund cards keep their normal border; a compact red due strip contains Due now beside View details when due; the included-commitment row uses the same red due treatment as loans, and Review focuses the fund. The themed Lump sum button sits to the right of Total P&L in Fund details; Pay SIP, Skip SIP, Edit fund, Delete fund, and automatically visible payment history belong to Fund details. Review scrolls to and focuses the fund without adding a card outline. A scheduled SIP cannot be paid or skipped before its due day. `POST /api/web/mutual-funds/{id}/sip-occurrences/{month}/skip` records a `SKIPPED` occurrence with the decision date, retaining the planned amount but no investment amount, NAV, units, or penalty. `PATCH /api/web/mutual-funds/{id}/plan` accepts `{ frequency?, effectiveMonth?, currentNav? }`; frequency is `MONTHLY` or `QUARTERLY`, the effective month must be future, and current NAV is a positive user supplied valuation. Scheme identity is immutable. Quarterly revisions preserve prior payment facts and place the next payment three months after the previous monthly payment. Detail history includes skipped SIPs with their due and skipped dates. SIP payment requires a due occurrence and records actual amount, date, and user-entered units; NAV is derived as amount divided by units without changing the planned SIP amount. After payment, Fund details reloads, removes its red due state, and shows the new transaction in payment history immediately.
